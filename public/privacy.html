Privacy Policy

ChoiceWise AI is designed to help users check suspicious messages while encouraging safer online behaviour.

What we store

We store your account details, scan category, risk score, red flags, recommendation, and a shortened message preview.

What users should not paste

Users should not paste passwords, OTPs, banking details, card numbers, private IDs, or highly sensitive personal information.

Message privacy

ChoiceWise hides common sensitive items like emails, phone numbers, and long numbers before saving scan previews.

Your control

Users can delete individual scans or clear their scan history from the dashboard.

AI analysis

When AI analysis is enabled, the message may be sent to an AI provider for risk analysis. Users should avoid entering sensitive private details.
