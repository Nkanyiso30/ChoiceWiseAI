About ChoiceWise AI

ChoiceWise AI helps users think carefully before they trust a message, accept an offer, buy online, sign a document, or make an important decision.

The platform checks suspicious messages and gives a risk score, red flags, safe actions, and a simple recommendation.

What ChoiceWise can help with

  • Suspicious job offers
  • Online store messages
  • WhatsApp or SMS scams
  • Email phishing
  • Contract or document risk explanation
  • General decision support

Important note

ChoiceWise AI is a decision support tool. It does not replace professional legal, financial, or security advice.
